• Web & E-Commerce Development. Building responsive, mobile-first websites and online stores using platforms such as WordPress, Shopify, and Squarespace. • Search Engine Optimization (SEO). Comprehensive organic growth strategies including technical SEO audits, keyword research, link building, content

optimization, and specialized e-commerce, local, and international SEO.
