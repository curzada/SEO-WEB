Working in the Philippines requires a 9(G) Pre-arranged Employment Visa for long-term roles, usually valid for 1–3 years (extendable). The process requires a local employer to petition for the worker, obtain an Alien Employment Permit (AEP) from DOLE, and secure approval from the Bureau of Immigration. A Provisional Work Permit (PWP) can be used while waiting for the 9(G) visa. ..
