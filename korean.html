Essential Grammar

Master these fundamental Korean grammar patterns to form sentences and communicate effectively.

1. Topic Marker -은/는 (eun/neun)

Used to mark the topic of a sentence. Use -은 after consonants and -는 after vowels.

저는 학생입니다.
Jeoneun haksaengimnida.
I am a student. (As for me, I am a student.)
이것은 책입니다.
Igeoseun chaekimnida.
This is a book.

2. Subject Marker -이/가 (i/ga)

Used to mark the subject of a sentence. Use -이 after consonants and -가 after vowels.

비가 옵니다.
Biga omnida.
It is raining. (Rain is falling.)
친구가 왔습니다.
Chinguga watseumnida.
A friend came.

3. Object Marker -을/를 (eul/reul)

Used to mark the object of a sentence. Use -을 after consonants and -를 after vowels.

저는 밥을 먹습니다.
Jeoneun babeul meokseumnida.
I eat rice/a meal.
책을 읽습니다.
Chaegeul ikseumnida.
I read a book.

4. Polite Ending -ㅂ니다/습니다 (-mnida/seumnida)

Formal polite ending used in declarative sentences. Use -ㅂ니다 after vowels and -습니다 after consonants.

갑니다
gamnida
go(es)
먹습니다
meokseumnida
eat(s)

5. Question Ending -ㅂ니까/습니까? (-mnikka/seumnikka?)

Formal polite ending used in questions. Use -ㅂ니까 after vowels and -습니까 after consonants.

갑니까?
gamnikka?
Do you go? / Are you going?
먹습니까?
meokseumnikka?
Do you eat? / Are you eating?

Exam Preparation Tips

Follow these strategies to excel in your Korean language exams!

📚 Study Strategies

  • Practice Hangul daily until you can read it fluently
  • Learn vocabulary in context, not just isolated words
  • Focus on high-frequency words first
  • Review grammar patterns regularly
  • Use spaced repetition for long-term retention

🎯 Listening Skills

  • Listen to Korean podcasts and news
  • Watch Korean dramas with subtitles
  • Practice with Korean language learning apps
  • Focus on distinguishing similar sounds
  • Take notes while listening to practice

✍️ Writing Practice

  • Practice writing Hangul characters daily
  • Keep a journal in simple Korean
  • Write summaries of what you've learned
  • Practice proper stroke order
  • Get feedback from native speakers if possible

🗣️ Speaking Skills

  • Practice pronunciation aloud
  • Record yourself and compare with native speakers
  • Learn common phrases and greetings
  • Practice shadowing techniques
  • Don't be afraid to make mistakes

📝 Exam Day Tips

  • Get enough sleep the night before
  • Arrive early to the exam center
  • Read all instructions carefully
  • Manage your time wisely
  • Review your answers if time permits

🔄 Regular Practice

  • Set aside dedicated study time each day
  • Use language exchange platforms
  • Join Korean language study groups
  • Take practice tests regularly
  • Track your progress and celebrate milestones
