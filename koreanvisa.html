South Korea Visa Application Guide

Your comprehensive resource for understanding Korean visa requirements, application procedures, and processing times. Navigate the visa process with confidence.

Visa System Overview

South Korea offers various visa types depending on the purpose of your visit. The Korean visa system is designed to facilitate legitimate travel while maintaining national security. Understanding which visa category applies to your situation is the first step in the application process.

✅ Important Note

Visa requirements may vary based on your nationality and purpose of visit. Always verify the most current information with the nearest Korean embassy or consulate before applying. This guide provides general information but may not reflect the most recent policy changes.

Who Needs a Visa?

Most foreign nationals require a visa to enter South Korea. However, citizens from certain countries enjoy visa-free entry for short-term visits (usually up to 90 days) for tourism or business purposes. Check the official list of visa-exempt countries on the Korean Ministry of Foreign Affairs website.

Basic Requirements

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Completed visa application form
  • Passport-sized photos (3.5cm x 4.5cm)
  • Proof of financial means
  • Travel itinerary or invitation letter (if applicable)

Types of Korean Visas

South Korea offers multiple visa categories to accommodate different purposes of visit. Each visa type has specific requirements, validity periods, and conditions of stay.

🛂 C-3

Short-Term Visit

Duration: Up to 90 days

For tourism, visiting relatives, business meetings, or conferences. Most common visa for short-term visitors.

  • Single or multiple entries available
  • Cannot engage in paid activities
  • Requires financial proof
  • Processing time: 5-10 business days
💼 D-8

Corporate Investment

Duration: 1-3 years

For foreign investors establishing or managing businesses in Korea. Requires substantial investment capital.

  • Minimum investment: KRW 100 million
  • Can sponsor dependents
  • Work permit included
  • Renewable
👨‍🎓 D-2

Study Abroad

Duration: Up to 2 years

For students enrolled in Korean universities, graduate schools, or language programs. Requires admission letter.

  • Must maintain full-time enrollment
  • Part-time work permitted with restrictions
  • Requires proof of financial support
  • Academic performance requirements
👨‍💼 E-7

Specially Designated Activities

Duration: 1-3 years

For professionals in specific fields including engineering, IT, and other specialized occupations.

  • Requires job offer from Korean company
  • Specific skill requirements
  • Work permit included
  • Renewable
👨‍🏭 E-9

Non-Professional Employment

Duration: 4 years 10 months

For foreign workers in designated sectors (fishing, agriculture, manufacturing, construction).

  • Government quota system
  • Must pass language test
  • Employer sponsorship required
  • Cannot change employers freely
👨‍👩‍👧 F-2

Resident

Duration: 3 years

For long-term residents including marriage immigrants, ethnic Koreans, and permanent residence candidates.

  • Freedom of employment
  • Can sponsor family members
  • Path to permanent residence
  • Multiple eligibility criteria
🏠 F-1

Visiting & Reunion

Duration: 1-2 years

For family members of Korean citizens or residents, including spouses and children.

  • Cannot engage in paid employment
  • Can apply for work permit
  • Renewable
  • Family relationship proof required
⛪ D-6

Cultural & Religious

Duration: 1-2 years

For religious workers, artists, journalists, and others in cultural fields.

  • Requires Korean organization sponsorship
  • Specific qualifications needed
  • Work permit included
  • Renewable

Application Process

Follow these steps to apply for your Korean visa. The process varies slightly depending on the visa type and your location.

1

Determine Visa Type

Identify which visa category matches your purpose of visit. Research specific requirements for your chosen visa type.

2

Gather Documents

Collect all required documents including passport, photos, application form, and supporting materials specific to your visa type.

3

Complete Application

Fill out the visa application form accurately. Ensure all information matches your supporting documents.

4

Pay Fees

Pay the visa application fee at the embassy/consulate or authorized visa application center. Keep the receipt.

5

Submit Application

Submit your application in person at the Korean embassy, consulate, or authorized visa center. Some countries allow mail applications.

6

Wait for Processing

Processing times vary from 5-15 business days depending on visa type and embassy workload. Some cases require additional review.

7

Collect Visa

Once approved, collect your passport with the visa sticker. Verify all details are correct before leaving the embassy.

8

Prepare for Travel

Book flights, arrange accommodation, and prepare for your trip. Ensure you have all necessary documents for immigration.

⚠️ Important Processing Notes

Processing times may be longer during peak seasons (summer, winter holidays, Korean holidays). Apply well in advance of your planned travel date. Some applications may require additional processing time if consular officials request more information or an interview.

Required Documents

Document requirements vary by visa type. Below is a general list of commonly required documents. Always check specific requirements for your visa category.

Basic Required Documents (All Visa Types)

📋 Core Documents

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Completed visa application form
  • Passport-sized photo (3.5cm x 4.5cm, white background, recent)
  • Copy of passport information page
  • Visa application fee receipt

Additional Documents by Visa Type

🛂 Tourist Visa (C-3) Additional Documents

  • Flight itinerary or round-trip ticket reservation
  • Hotel reservation or accommodation details
  • Proof of financial means (bank statements for last 3 months)
  • Travel itinerary or tour schedule
  • Employment verification or proof of business (if applicable)

💼 Business Visa Additional Documents

  • Invitation letter from Korean company
  • Business registration certificate of Korean company
  • Proof of business relationship or purpose
  • Company profile and business card
  • Proof of financial support

👨‍🎓 Student Visa (D-2) Additional Documents

  • Letter of admission from Korean educational institution
  • Proof of enrollment or acceptance
  • Financial support proof (bank statements, scholarship letters)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Study plan or statement of purpose
  • Proof of language proficiency (if required)

👨‍💼 Work Visa (E-series) Additional Documents

  • Employment contract with Korean company
  • Letter of guarantee from employer
  • Business registration of Korean employer
  • Copies of professional qualifications and certificates
  • Work experience verification
  • Resume/CV

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Visa Additional Documents

  • Marriage certificate (if spouse visa)
  • Birth certificate (if child visa)
  • Proof of relationship (photos, correspondence)
  • Korean sponsor's ID and registration
  • Proof of financial support from sponsor

Processing Times

⏰ Standard Processing Times

  • Tourist/Business Visa: 5-10 business days
  • Student Visa: 7-14 business days
  • Work Visa: 10-20 business days
  • Permanent Residence: 6-12 months
  • Expedited Service: 2-3 business days (additional fee applies)

⚠️ Fee Notes

Fees are subject to change and may vary by country. Fees are generally non-refundable even if the visa is denied. Some embassies accept local currency or have different fee structures. Always confirm current fees with the Korean embassy/consulate where you're applying.

E-Visa System

South Korea offers electronic visa (E-Visa) processing for certain visa types and nationalities. The E-Visa system streamlines the application process and reduces processing time.

✅ E-Visa Eligibility

E-Visa is available for select countries and visa types including short-term business, family visits, and some employment categories. Check the official Korean immigration website to see if you qualify for E-Visa application.

E-Visa Application Process

1

Online Application

Complete the online application form on the official Korean E-Visa website. Upload digital copies of required documents.

2

Pay Fee Online

Pay the visa application fee securely using credit card or other accepted payment methods.

3

Track Application

Monitor your application status online using the reference number provided after submission.

4

Receive E-Visa

Once approved, download and print your E-Visa. Present it along with your passport upon arrival in Korea.

💡 E-Visa Benefits

  • Convenient online application from anywhere
  • Faster processing times (3-5 business days)
  • No need to visit embassy/consulate in person
  • Easy tracking of application status
  • Secure payment processing
  • Reduced paperwork

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Korean visa applications and requirements.

Q: How long before my travel date should I apply for a Korean visa?
A: It's recommended to apply at least 4-6 weeks before your planned travel date, especially during peak seasons. This allows time for processing and any potential additional requests from the embassy.
Q: Can I extend my visa while in Korea?
A: Some visa types can be extended within Korea at the immigration office. Generally, tourist visas cannot be extended beyond the initial period. Contact local immigration for specific extension requirements.
Q: What if my visa application is rejected?
A: Visa decisions are final and generally cannot be appealed. However, you can reapply after addressing the reasons for rejection. Review the rejection notice carefully and provide stronger documentation in your next application.
Q: Do I need a visa for transit through Korea?
A: Most nationalities do not need a visa for airside transit (remaining in the international area). However, if you need to leave the airport or switch terminals, you may need a transit visa depending on your nationality.
Q: Can I work on a tourist visa?
A: No, tourist visas (C-3) do not permit any form of paid work. Working on a tourist visa is illegal and can result in deportation, fines, and being barred from future entry to Korea.
Q: How much money do I need to show for financial proof?
A: While there's no fixed amount, you should demonstrate sufficient funds for your entire stay. A general guideline is showing $1,000-2,000 per month, plus funds for return airfare and accommodation.
Q: Can I bring family members on my work visa?
A: Yes, most long-term work visas allow you to sponsor dependent family members (spouse and unmarried children under 20). They can apply for dependent visas (F-1) and may eventually qualify for their own work permits.
Q: What documents are accepted as proof of financial means?
A: Bank statements for the last 3-6 months, employment letters, income tax returns, property ownership documents, scholarship letters (for students), and sponsorship letters with proof of sponsor's financial status.

Tips & Resources

Follow these tips to increase your chances of a successful visa application and ensure a smooth process.

💡 Application Tips

  • Apply Early: Submit your application well in advance of your travel date
  • Be Accurate: Ensure all information on forms matches your documents exactly
  • Provide Complete Documentation: Submit all required documents and additional supporting materials
  • Use Recent Documents: Documents should generally be no older than 3 months
  • Translate Documents: All documents in languages other than Korean or English must be translated
  • Be Honest: Provide truthful information. Misrepresentation can lead to permanent bans
  • Prepare for Interview: Some visa types require an interview. Dress professionally and be prepared to answer questions about your visit
