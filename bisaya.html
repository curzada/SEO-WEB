    Grammar Basics

    Personal Pronouns

    • Ako - I
    • Ikaw - You
    • Siya - He/She
    • Kita - We (inclusive)
    • Kami - We (exclusive)
    • Sila - They

    Personal Markers

    • Ang - The (focus marker)
    • Ng - Of (non-focus marker)
    • Sa - In/At/To
      • Example:
      Ang bata = The child
      Ng bata = Of the child
      Sa bata = To the child

    Basic Sentence Structure

    • Verb-Subject-Object
    • Nagkaon ako sa pagkaon
    • I ate the food
      • Common Verbs:
      Kaon = Eat
      Tulog = Sleep
      Adto = Go

    Question Words

    • Unsa - What
    • Asa - Where
    • Kanus-a - When
    • Kinsa - Who
    • Ngano - Why
    • Pila - How many/much
